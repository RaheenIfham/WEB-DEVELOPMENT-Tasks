Admission Steps

  1. Submit an online application form through our secure admissions portal.
  2. Upload official high school transcripts and standardized test scores.
  3. Provide two letters of recommendation from academic mentors.
  4. Attend a virtual panel interview with the department faculty.
  5. Receive your official admission decision and secure your enrollment deposit.
